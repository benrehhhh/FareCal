How fares are calculated

FareCal uses the fare rules configured for each transportation type. Two common structures are supported:

  • Base fare + succeeding kilometer — a base fare covers the first few kilometers, then a per-kilometer rate applies beyond that.
  • Per-kilometer rate — the distance is simply multiplied by a per-kilometer rate, with an optional minimum fare.

The specified passenger type determines the applicable discount (e.g. 20% for students, senior citizens, and PWDs). Fare amounts are rounded according to the rounding rule configured for the transportation type.

Disclaimer: FareCal provides estimated fare calculations based on the fare rates configured in the system. Actual fares may vary depending on applicable transportation regulations, route conditions, fare adjustments, and other authorized charges.
